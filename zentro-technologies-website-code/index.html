How We Work

A clear process, from first call to ongoing support

No surprises. You know what happens at each stage — and what we need from you.

  1. STEP 01→

    Discover

    We learn about your business, your customers and what the project needs to achieve.

  2. STEP 02→

    Plan

    We agree the scope, structure and content — in writing — before any building starts.

  3. STEP 03→

    Design

    You see the design direction early, and we refine it together until it represents your business properly.

  4. STEP 04→

    Build

    We build the site or tool cleanly and responsively, testing on real phones, tablets and desktops.

  5. STEP 05→

    Launch

    We launch carefully, check everything works, and hand over with a clear explanation of what you have.

  6. STEP 06✓

    Support

    We stay available for maintenance, fixes and improvements as your business grows.
